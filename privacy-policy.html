Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 26, 2026

Verbode does not sell your personal data. We collect only what is needed to run the app and process payments. We never use your data for advertising.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Verbode ("we," "us," or "our") collects when you use the Verbode mobile application, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. If you have questions, email us at shayanwork612@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information
When you sign in, we collect your email address through Firebase Authentication (Google). We do not store your password — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase.

Usage data
As you use the app, we record:

  • Problems you have attempted and their outcomes
  • Your scores across six performance dimensions
  • Session duration and timing data
  • Your problem history and progress over time

Payment information
If you subscribe to Verbode Pro, your payment is processed by RevenueCat and the relevant app store (Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not see or store your credit card number. We receive only your subscription status (active, expired, cancelled).

Device information
We receive basic device identifiers through Firebase and RevenueCat for the purpose of managing your session and subscription.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your account
  • Display your session history, scores, and stats
  • Process and verify your subscription status
  • Improve the quality and content of the app
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregated usage statistics may be retained indefinitely as they cannot identify you.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights:

Access Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
Deletion Ask us to delete your account and personal data.
Correction Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
Portability Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
Opt-out of sale We do not sell data — this right is always satisfied.
Withdraw consent Stop using the app and request deletion at any time.

To exercise any right, email shayanwork612@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. EU residents have additional rights under GDPR and may lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

6. Children's Privacy

Verbode is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit. Authentication tokens and passwords are managed by Firebase and never stored by us directly. Payment data is handled exclusively by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat — we never see raw payment details.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We work to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users

Verbode is operated from the United States. If you access the app from the European Union or other regions with data protection laws, your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using Verbode, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
